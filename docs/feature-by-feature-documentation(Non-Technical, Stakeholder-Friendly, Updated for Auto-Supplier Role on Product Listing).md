# Feature-by-Feature Documentation

This document provides a non-technical, stakeholder-friendly overview of the features in the Cheating Daddy application.

## Core Features

-   **Live AI Assistance**: Get real-time help from an AI assistant during your video calls and meetings.
-   **Screen and Audio Capture**: The AI assistant can see what's on your screen and hear what's being said, so it can provide relevant and contextual help.
-   **Multiple Profiles**: Choose from a variety of AI assistant profiles, including Interview, Sales Call, Business Meeting, Presentation, and Negotiation.
-   **Transparent Overlay**: The application window is always on top, so you can see the AI assistant's suggestions without having to switch between windows.
-   **Click-through Mode**: Make the application window transparent to clicks, so you can interact with the content behind it.
-   **Cross-platform**: The application works on macOS, Windows, and Linux.

## New Features

### Colleague Profile

The new "Colleague" profile provides a professional and collaborative AI assistant. It's like having a helpful coworker by your side, ready to provide feedback on your ideas, documents, and other work.

### Conversation Timestamps

Each turn in your conversation history is now timestamped, so you can easily see when each message was sent. This makes it easier to review your past conversations and track the progress of your discussions.
